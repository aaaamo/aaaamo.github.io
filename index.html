About

I am a master's student in Computational Design at Carnegie Mellon University. I received my Bachelor of Architecture from Seoul National University and worked for several years building software for designers. I am interested in how a designer sees a design while working on it. My current work uses neural rendering and AR to give visual feedback that keeps up with each edit and shows only what is in the model.
